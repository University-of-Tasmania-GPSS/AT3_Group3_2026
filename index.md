<h1 align="center">Karst Groundwater Vulnerability in Tasmania</h1>

This book presents a GIS project assessing relative karst groundwater vulnerability in Tasmania. It brings together three factors: karst susceptibility, hydrological connectivity, and land-use pressure.

The workflow is developed first at Mole Creek and then applied across Tasmania at a coarser scale. Comparing the local and statewide results helps show how scale and resolution affect the patterns identified.
